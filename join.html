Your flight companion

FLY WITH GROUP ACARS.

ECV pilots use British Midland Group ACARS for SimBrief import, dispatch briefings, flight tracking and PIREP submission. Your flight history brings completed sectors, times and report statuses together in one place.

Installation and access guidance will be provided through Group onboarding. The official joining link and ECV entry requirements are still awaiting confirmation.
