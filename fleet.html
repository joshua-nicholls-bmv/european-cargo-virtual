Featured aircraft

AIRBUS
A340.

An Airbus cargo fleet for scheduled, charter and seasonal operations.

Our primary cargo aircraft are the Airbus A340-600F and A340-500F. Aerosoft and iniBuilds A340 add-ons are also accepted for ECV flying in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. All A340 variants may operate the 28 original scheduled sectors. Three A321Fs operate our new European return routes: Cardiff–Frankfurt, Bournemouth–Liège, Birmingham–Billund and Teesside–Cologne.
