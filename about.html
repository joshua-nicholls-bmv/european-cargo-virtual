SPECIALIST CARGO.

European Cargo Virtual is a virtual cargo airline for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, focused on realistic Airbus A340 operations, long-haul routes and specialist cargo flying.

From Cardiff, Bournemouth, Birmingham and Teesside, we fly scheduled cargo services alongside charter operations and seasonal flights.

As part of the British Midland Group, ECV pilots have access to shared systems, ACARS, flight tracking, pilot progression and wider Group activities. ECV retains its own dedicated cargo identity and community.
