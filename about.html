Our identity

CARGO FLYING.
COMMUNITY SPIRIT.

European Cargo Virtual is a flight simulation community inspired by the world of commercial air freight.

Our first release focuses on the Airbus A340 and the rewards of thoughtful long-haul flying. We are building a place for pilots to enjoy the aircraft, share experience and approach each mission with care.

As a sister VA to British Midland Virtual, we share a commitment to a welcoming community and a clear, professional flying experience.
