British Midland Group ACARS

ONE GROUP.
ONE FLIGHT PLATFORM.

European Cargo Virtual uses British Midland Group ACARS for dispatch preparation, simulator flight tracking and pilot reports.

Bring your cargo sector into the Group’s shared flying experience: import a SimBrief plan, review the dispatch briefing, connect to Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 and record your flight.

01

Prepare your flight

Create your flight plan in SimBrief, then import it into ACARS using your SimBrief user ID. Review the dispatch briefing and check the route, aircraft and registration before departure.

02

Connect and record

Connect ACARS to your simulator and start your flight recording. Follow the flight phase, timer, aircraft details and event log throughout the sector.

03

Submit and review

After completing your flight, review and submit the pilot report (PIREP). Flight History keeps your submitted sectors together with aircraft, registrations, flight times, landing rates, scores and report statuses.

ACARS access and installation guidance are provided through the Group’s pilot onboarding. ECV-specific SOPs, reporting rules and support contacts will be published separately.

OPERATIONS DESK.

Which simulators and aircraft will be supported?

Our simulator is Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. The fleet includes the Airbus A340-600, A340-600F and A340-500. Approved add-ons and livery downloads will be confirmed separately.

How do dispatch and flight reporting work?

ECV uses British Midland Group ACARS. Import your SimBrief flight plan, review the dispatch briefing, connect to MSFS 2024 and start flight recording. After the sector, review and submit your PIREP, then check its status in Flight History. ECV’s detailed reporting rules are TBA.

Where do I get ACARS?

The Group’s pilot onboarding provides ACARS installation and access guidance. A confirmed public download or onboarding link will be added here when supplied.

Does this website show live ACARS flights?

ACARS records flights through the Group platform. This ECV website currently presents the fleet, schedules and operating guidance; a live ACARS feed has not yet been connected here.

Where can I find the operating handbook?

Standard operating procedures (SOPs) are TBA. Until published, use your aircraft’s official simulator documentation for procedures and limitations.
