The cargo workflow

BEFORE. DURING. AFTER.

A simple framework for an enjoyable, well-prepared simulator flight.

01

Plan the mission

Review the route, weather, airport facilities and aircraft documentation. Allow enough time for the sector you want to fly.

02

Prepare the departure

Check the load and fuel in your simulator, follow the aircraft’s procedures and make time for a departure briefing.

03

Fly and review

Manage the flight, prepare the arrival and take a moment afterwards to review what went well and what you learned.
