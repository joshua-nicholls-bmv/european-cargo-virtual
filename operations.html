British Midland Group ACARS

FROM FLIGHT PLAN
TO PILOT REPORT.

Choose. Plan. Brief. Connect. Fly. Report.

Sign in to load your pilot profile, rank and statistics, then follow these six steps for your ECV flight.

  1. Choose

    Sign in to ACARS and open My Flight. Search for your departure and arrival by airport name or ICAO, then choose an available fleet aircraft. Scheduled flight numbers fill automatically. For other operations, choose “Custom flight · BMA Tech Ops / European Cargo Virtual”.

  2. Plan

    Select Plan in SimBrief. Review the route, flight number, aircraft and registration, then check fuel, payload, weather and alternates before generating your plan. Return to ACARS and import the generated plan; ACARS checks it against your selected flight and aircraft.

  3. Brief

    Read the dispatch briefing, including flight details, planned weights, fuel, route and operations or airport notices. Expand the SimBrief NOTAM sections as needed. Acknowledge the briefing to unlock simulator connection and flight start. You can reopen it using View Briefing.

  4. Connect

    Load the correct aircraft and registration in MSFS 2024, then connect ACARS to the simulator.

  5. Fly

    Select Start Flight before departure. ACARS tracks your flight phases, timings, fuel use, landing performance and event log throughout the sector.

  6. Report

    After parking, complete the flight and confirm that your pilot report (PIREP) was submitted successfully. Review Flight History and My Statistics; eligible reports count towards statistics and community leaderboards.

Keep your briefing and report in check.

Acknowledge the briefing before connecting, start recording before departure and confirm successful PIREP submission after the flight.

Flight-plan and tracking notifications may appear in Discord when the Group’s notification setup is enabled. Detailed ECV SOPs and charter rules remain TBA.

OPERATIONS DESK.

Which simulators and aircraft will be supported?

We fly in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. Our primary cargo aircraft are the A340-600F and A340-500F, with Aerosoft and iniBuilds A340 add-ons also accepted. All A340 variants may fly all 28 scheduled sectors. Livery links will follow.

How do dispatch and flight reporting work?

Choose your flight in ACARS, plan in SimBrief and import the generated plan. Read and acknowledge the dispatch briefing, connect to MSFS 2024 and select Start Flight before departure. After parking, complete the flight and confirm successful PIREP submission. The six-step guide above covers the full workflow.

Where do I get ACARS?

The Group’s pilot onboarding provides ACARS installation and access guidance. Join the ECV Discord for application and onboarding guidance.

Does this website show live ACARS flights?

ACARS records flights through the Group platform. This ECV website currently presents the fleet, schedules and operating guidance; a live ACARS feed has not yet been connected here.

Where can I find the operating handbook?

Standard operating procedures (SOPs) are TBA. Until published, use your aircraft’s official simulator documentation for procedures and limitations.
